## BeefSC: A model for calculating optimal stocking rates and costs of beef

Together with Stefan Wirsenius, I developed BeefSC (Beef Stocking-rate and Cost model),
a spatially explicit framework for estimating optimal cattle stocking rates, beef yields,
and production costs across Brazil under four stylized beef production systems spanning
extensive to semi-intensive management. The model combines a biophysical stocking-rate
optimization module with an enterprise cost module, translating pasture growth, herd
dynamics, supplementation, and feedlot finishing into pixel-level cost and output estimates.

The report includes the full model description, equations and parameters, input datasets,
and results from the Brazilian application. Read the
[full report on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/18845417).
